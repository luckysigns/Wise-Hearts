Chinese face reading is a classical branch of Chinese medicine, not a mystical practice. Hilarey trained in the tradition taught by Lillian Pearl Bridges and the Lotus Institute — a system for reading constitutional patterns, emotional tendencies, and life themes written into the structure and features of your face. It's rooted in centuries of observation — not prediction, but deep recognition of who you are.
