Meet Hilarey

20 years of healing at the intersection of East and West

I'm Hilarey Boly — an acupuncturist, Chinese face reader, and content creator. For over two decades, I've worked with people through Five Element acupuncture, helping them heal and reconnect with who they truly are.

My face reading practice grows from the teachings of Lillian Pearl Bridges and the Lotus Institute — a tradition rooted in the Five Elements, Shen, and ancient Taoist wisdom. Now I'm bringing it beyond the clinic through Wise Hearts Connect.
