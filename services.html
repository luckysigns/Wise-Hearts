Face reading is the heart of Wise Hearts Connect — offered virtually so you can connect from anywhere. Hilarey's licensed acupuncture practice continues alongside it, in Portland. There's a place here for you, wherever you are.

The signature offering

Chinese Face Reading

Chinese face reading — the tradition Hilarey trained in through Lillian Pearl Bridges and the Lotus Institute — is one of the oldest self-knowledge systems in Chinese medicine. Your face holds a detailed map of your constitutional nature, emotional history, strengths, and the unique gifts you carry into this world.

Reading Hilarey's features through the lens of the Five Elements, she reveals patterns that often feel immediately, deeply true. Most people leave saying "I've never felt so seen." Choose the format that fits your life:

  • Virtual — a live 60-minute reading over video call, from anywhere in the world
  • In-person — sit with Hilarey in Portland for the full experience
  • Photo-based — send clear photos, receive a personal recorded reading to keep
  • Every format includes a written summary you can return to

Hilarey's clinical practice

Five Element Acupuncture

Alongside Wise Hearts Connect, Hilarey runs her licensed acupuncture practice in Portland. Five Element acupuncture is a classical form of Chinese medicine that treats the whole person — body, mind, and spirit — asking which Element is calling for attention right now.

In clinical practice since 2007, she brings genuine care, deep listening, and 17+ years of experience to every appointment — treating people across the full spectrum of life, from children to elders, and even a few four-legged friends.

  • Initial intake and comprehensive health history
  • Five Element constitutional diagnosis
  • Acupuncture treatment tailored to your current state
  • Possible integration of tuning forks (Acutonics) or vibrational essences
  • Ongoing care and seasonal tune-ups available

How it works

Your virtual reading, step by step

Distance is never a barrier to being seen. A virtual reading carries the same depth as sitting in the room together — many clients find the screen-to-screen intimacy adds something special. Here's exactly what to expect:

  • 1. Request your booking — choose virtual, share a few day and time preferences
  • 2. Confirm with Hilarey — she replies within 1–2 days to lock in your time
  • 3. Send your photos — a couple of clear, well-lit photos beforehand
  • 4. Your 60-minute session — a live, warm video conversation over Zoom
  • 5. Keep the insights — a written summary lands in your inbox afterward
